About us

At Saniclean, we are committed to our client's satisfaction. We strive to a earn your trust as well as your business. We've been in the commercial cleaning and maintenance industry for over 26 years.

 

Anna Cartagena, founder of Saniclean, is second generation in the cleaning and maintenance industry. Growing up, she learned the importance of an organized and clean business/home environment, and the difference that it makes in the way that it represents itself. In addition, she understands that living/working in a germ controlled environment is imperative.

 

At Saniclean, we strive to keep a good relationship with our customers and staff. In order to become part of the Saniclean team, all our staff are screened via thorough background checks with local law enforcement. They also are required to attend extensive ongoing training sessions that constantly re-envision them of the company's high standards.

 

We value our staff, and therefore, have a long-term working relationship with them. This relationship allows our customers a sense of security and trust, knowing who is working in their homes and place of business.

 

Most services are available on a two-hour notice. We also provide a 24 hour emergency calling service.
